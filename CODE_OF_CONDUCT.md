# Code of conduct

This is a small project maintained by one person in their spare time. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to make that easy for everyone else.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not with the person.
- Be specific. A report with what you did, what happened and what you expected helps; "it does not work" does not.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind, and nothing sexual or violent. Do not publish anyone's private information.
- Respect the maintainer's time and decisions. "No" and "not now" are answers.

Behaviour that breaks these rules can be reported privately to the maintainer through the contact on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/prabhask5). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who do not follow this.
